About Me关于我

I am an undergraduate student at University of Arizona, majoring in AI/CS. I am currently working on natural language processing (NLP) research at CLULab and advised by Prof. Mihai Surdeanu. I am also a research intern at SJTULUMIA Lab, advised by Prof. Zhouhan Lin. 我是就读于亚利桑那大学的本科生，主修人工智能/计算机科学。目前，我主要在CLULab进行自然语言处理（NLP）研究，有幸接受Mihai Surdeanu 教授的指导。同时我正在上海交通大学林洲汉教授的LUMIA Lab实习。

My general research interests lie at the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Information Retrieval (IR), with a specific focus on memory mechanisms in LLMs. I am especially interested in how retrieval can provide LLMs with effective external memory, and how LLMs can in turn improve retrieval and other components of modern IR systems. 我的研究兴趣集中在大语言模型 (LLMs) 与信息检索 (IR) 的交叉领域，重点关注LLM的记忆机制。我特别感兴趣的是，IR如何为LLM提供有效的外部记忆，以及LLM反过来又如何改进现代IR系统。

I am preparing for Fall 2027 Ph.D./Master applications in IR and NLP. I am also actively seeking internship opportunities in both academia and industry. 我正在寻找2027年秋季入学的博士或硕士项目机会（信息检索、NLP相关方向）。我也正积极寻求实习机会。
